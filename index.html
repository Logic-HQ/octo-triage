Less tab-hopping. More context.

Our backlog.
With a little perspective.

Whether you look after 5 repos or 200, the idea is the same: put the next useful action in front of you.

01 / GATHER

One place to catch up.

Bring open issues and pull requests from your selected repositories into a shared view. Four columns keep incoming work, reviews, stale threads, and blockers separate.

02 / MAKE SENSE OF IT

Not just newest first.

Use status, labels, and contribution history to find what needs a human. Spot a first contribution, an overdue review, or a thread that's gone quiet for 7+ days.

03 / KEEP THINGS MOVING

Less navigation. More action.

The project’s action workflow brings labeling, closing, merging, and review requests closer to the board, with keyboard-friendly controls. Available actions depend on your GitHub permissions.

Alongside GitHub, not in the middle

Your GitHub
stay yours.

OctoTriage is designed to talk to GitHub through its official GraphQL API, with bulk fetching that works within GitHub’s rate limits.

The architecture keeps your access token in your browser session and caches repository data on your device. No centralized application backend storing your tokens or code.

Access should stay scoped to the repositories you select and the actions you use. Read-only triage and write actions need different permissions—check the project’s setup guidance before connecting.

No third-party analytics. No repository-data marketplace.
